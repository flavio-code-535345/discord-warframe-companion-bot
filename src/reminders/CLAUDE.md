# Reminders

Notes for this folder. The rules that apply everywhere, the commands and the list of the other notes are in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**Reminders** (`src/reminders/service.ts`, `parse.ts`, `/remindme set|list|cancel` in `src/discord/commands/remind.ts`; table `reminders`, migration with epoch-ms `due_at`/`created_at`, `attempts`; `test/reminders.test.ts`): a member's own note, DM'd at the time they pick.
- **Time** (`parseReminderTime`, pure, no network): a length from now ("2h", "3 days", "1w 2d", "in 90 minutes"; units s/m/h/d/w, no months or years), `tomorrow [time]` (09:00 by default), a bare time of day (the next one), `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`, or a day-first date the German way (`24.12.2026`, `24.12.26`, `24-12-26`, `24/12/26`, optionally with `20:00` or `20 Uhr`; an impossible day such as 31.02 is refused). Anything without a zone is read in `CLAN_TIMEZONE` (`zonedEpoch` in `time.ts`, DST aware); an explicit `Z` or offset is kept. `/remindme set` says which zone it used.
- **Limits:** 25 pending per member (`MAX_PENDING`), message up to 300 characters, at least 1 minute ahead, at most 2 years. All refusals are `UserError`.
- **Delivery** (the `reminders` job, every 30 s, `remindersJob`): a DM with `allowedMentions: { parse: [] }` and the text through `neutralise` (the member's words are shown, never acted on). When DMs are closed (50007), or a DM failed a third time, Ordis replies in the channel the reminder was set in, pinging only that member, then drops it; no channel means it is dropped. Other failures count in `attempts` and are retried until `MAX_ATTEMPTS` (3). A reminder is deleted once sent or given up, so never twice. After downtime an overdue one goes out once, saying "sorry, I am late" (`LATE_MS`).
- No snooze button: a DM carries only link buttons (see `src/discord/CLAUDE.md`).

# Self-assign roles

Notes for this folder. The rules that apply everywhere, the commands and the list of the other notes are in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**Self-assign roles** (`src/selfroles/service.ts`, `/roles add|remove|post|list`, MCP `set_self_role`, `list_self_roles`, `post_roles_panel`; table `self_roles`): one panel message with a button per role (`roles:<roleId>`, a tap toggles, ephemeral answer), at most 20 (`MAX_SELF_ROLES`: Discord allows five rows of five buttons, and the last row is the notifications). Groups keep the order they were added in (`position`); a group without descriptions is a compact inline field (Platform and Region side by side), one with descriptions is full width, and each group's buttons start their own row (packed together only when that would need more than four rows, `MAX_ROLE_ROWS`). The panel's last field and last row are not roles but the DM notifications: the buttons `notify:clanEvent`, `notify:squad`, `notify:weekly` and `feeds:mine`, and links to the board channels with their "DM me when…" menus (Notifications in `src/discord/CLAUDE.md`). Roles say who someone is; Ordis pings none of them, and the six ping roles that used to be offered here are gone. `rolesPanel` reads the settings for those links, so its `ctx` needs a store. `assertSelfAssignable` (checked on add AND every tap) refuses @everyone, managed roles, roles at or above Ordis, roles with `DANGEROUS` permissions (`src/discord/permissions.ts`, shared with the screenshot roles), staff and clan rank names, and earned mastery/level roles. The panel is kept with `src/discord/live-message.ts` (`postLive` moves the message and deletes the old copy, `editLive` edits in place and forgets a deleted one; bot_meta `roles_panel`). The welcome greeting links the role channel found by name (`🎭┃roles`).

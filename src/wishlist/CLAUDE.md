# Prime wishlist

Notes for this folder. The rules that apply everywhere, the commands and the list of the other notes are in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**The wishlist** (`plan.ts`, `service.ts`, `src/discord/commands/wishlist.ts`, migration 40 `wishlist`, `test/wishlist.test.ts`): a member lists the prime sets they are collecting (`/wishlist add · show · list · remove`, at most `MAX_WISHES` = 20) and ticks the parts they already have. Everything a member sees is ephemeral. An entry is the set's name as the relic book has it (`findSet` reads "saryn" or "Saryn Prime Set"; the table compares names without case) plus `have`, a JSON list of part names; a set no relic holds (`inRelics`) is refused.

**The reckoning** (`plan.ts`, pure): `wishPlan(book, set, have, owned)` gives every part with its price, ducats, the relics that hold it (the ones that still drop first, from the relic book's `sets`) and, when `owned` is known, the copies the member owns of each by refinement; `missingPlat` counts a part the set needs twice twice, `unpriced` the missing parts without a price, `ownedRelics` the owned copies that hold a part still missing. A part that is a weapon of its own (Bronco Prime for Akbronco Prime) has no relics and is priced as that weapon's set (`ownSet`). `varziaOffer(setName, ws, now)` says what Varzia sells of a set: the set under its own name, a pack that names it, and the relics of its resurgence, which the feed names after the vault's Warframes ("Lith relic · Nidus Saryn A"); the name is matched as whole words, so Magnus Prime is not Mag Prime, and sigils or statues that carry the name do not count. A weapon the feed lists under an internal name is missed.

**The cards** (`service.ts`): `wishCard` (one set: what is still needed with prices, relics, "You own …", the parts already there, a `wish:have:<wishId>` menu that ticks parts, and a line saying why owned relics are not marked when they are not) and `wishListCard` (every set in a line or two). The member's relics come through `relicsOf` (`src/alecaframe/access.ts`: `ok`, `unlinked`, `unshared`, `unreadable`; never throws), the Varzia line from the world state when it is on. `handleWishSelect` (routed in `bot.ts` by the `wish:` prefix) stores the ticked parts and redraws the card with `interaction.update`, so what it reads from outside gets 1.5 s. The commands answer through `replyCard` (`src/discord/card-reply.ts`). `wishChoices` is the autocomplete: sets not yet on the list to add, the member's own to remove, their own first to show.

**The DMs:** `notifyWishlistVarzia` (every `world-state` round) tells a member once per visit when Varzia sells sets on their list, all of them in one DM (`seen_worldstate` keys `wish:<wishId>:varzia:<when her stock changes>`; `/wishlist add` marks the key when its card already said it). `notifyWishlistVault` is called by the vault watch (`src/discord/vault-watch.ts`) with the sets that left or re-entered the drop tables, with or without a news channel. Both go through `sendDm`, so closed DMs are logged and nothing throws. `get_bot_health.wishlist` counts the sets and the members.

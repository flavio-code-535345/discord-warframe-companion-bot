# Leveling

Notes for this folder. The rules that apply everywhere, the commands and the list of the other notes are in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**Leveling** (`src/leveling/`, `src/discord/commands/levels.ts`): Arcane/MEE6-style XP, on by default (`/setup levels [channel]` to choose the announcement channel, `/setup off feature:Leveling` to disable; stored as `levelingOff`/`levelChannelId`). `xp.ts` is pure: a level costs `5n²+50n+100` XP, a message earns 15-25 XP with a 60 s cooldown, voice earns 10 XP/min, and `planLevelRoles` picks the highest existing "Level N+" role at or below the level (matched by name via `levelTier`) and removes the other tiers, skipping managed roles and roles above Ordis. Only total XP is stored (`member_xp`); the level is derived. `service.ts` has `handleLevelMessage` (a separate call in the `MessageCreate` handler, ignoring bots, the honeypot/screenshot/riven channels, clean-sticky channels and `/xp ignore` channels), the level-up announcement and `awardVoiceXp` (the `voice-xp` job: members in a voice channel with another human, not deafened or muted, not AFK). Voice XP never touches the message cooldown. Commands: `/rank [member]`, `/leaderboard [page]`, and staff `/xp give|setlevel|reset|ignore|sync`. **Level 10 reward:** AttachFiles + EmbedLinks live only on the server roles Level 10+…60+ and on leadership/staff roles (Leadership, Rat Lord/King/Count), not on `Clan` or `@everyone`; because members hold one tier role, every new tier must carry both. The media channel and the ss-roles channel (attachments) override this for @everyone. The announcement adds a note when level 10 is crossed (`MEDIA_LEVEL`).

# Squads and voice rooms

Notes for this folder. The rules that apply everywhere, the commands and the list of the other notes are in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**Squad voice and activities** (`src/lfg/`): with `/setup lfgvoice`, `/lfg` creates a voice channel per squad (`voice.ts`). `lifecycle.ts` holds the pure rules (`sweepAction`: expiry, host-absent grace period, delete a closed squad's channel once empty), so they're testable without Discord. The once-a-minute `squad-voice` job (`sweepVoice`) applies them, which survives restarts and needs no per-channel timers; it reads who is in a channel from the cache, so the (unprivileged) GuildVoiceStates intent is required. `hostAbsentSince` starts at creation, so a host who never joins voice still ends the squad. The `activities` table (case-insensitive name, image URL, fixed channel; its ping role went with migration 38) feeds `renderLfg` (thumbnail) and the channel a squad is posted in; activities without a row still work. `squads.ts` `createSquad` is the one way a squad gets posted (`/lfg` calls it): in the activity's own channel, else where it was asked for, else in `lfgChannelId` (`/setup lfg`, MCP `update_bot_settings lfg_channel_id`); it creates the voice channel when squad voice is set up (one open voice squad per host), stores and posts the squad with its Join/Leave/Close buttons, and DMs the members with a `squad` tracker (`notifySquad`, never the host). Nobody is pinged in the channel; a post that fails closes the squad and removes its voice channel again. `joinSquad` is the one way a member is added outside the Join button's own answer (seat in the store, the post redrawn, voice access, and `squadFullNotice`, the "Squad full" message that pings only the squad's own members, when that join filled it); the Join button posts the same notice.

**Squad up** (`arbitration-squad.ts`, the `feeds:squad` button on the Arbitration board and on the `/now` card, `test/squad-up.test.ts`): one tap and the member is in a squad for the Arbitration. `squadSlot` picks the hour (pure): the one running, or the next one when under 15 minutes are left and the schedule is trusted. The first tap of an hour starts the squad through `createSquad` (activity `Arbitrations`, so that activity's picture, channel and the `squad` DMs apply; 4 seats; details from `slotDetails`; it expires when the Arbitration ends), the next taps join it (`joinSquad`), and a full or closed squad makes room for a new one. bot_meta `arby_squads` remembers which post belongs to which hour (kept 3 hours; a record that cannot be read is no squad). `squadUpForArbitration` runs one tap at a time (a promise chain), so two members tapping in the same second get one squad. The button is only drawn when `lfgChannelId` is set; `squadUpReply` (feeds/handlers.ts) is what the member is told, with the voice channel and its grace period for the host.

`autovc.ts` is join-to-create (`/setup autovc`): `handleVoiceStateUpdate` (a `VoiceStateUpdate` listener) makes a room per member in the lobby's category and deletes it when empty; rooms are tracked in `temp_voice` and `sweepTempVoice` (same minute timer) removes empty or vanished ones, sparing rooms younger than 30s so the owner can be moved in. `/vc` is owner-only and checks `temp_voice`. Image URLs must pass `validImageUrl` (plain https), which is why the admin panel's CSP allows `img-src https:`.

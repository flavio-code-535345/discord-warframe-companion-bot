# Wiki index

Notes for this folder. The rules that apply everywhere, the commands and the list of the other notes are in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**Wiki index** (`src/wiki/`): `crawler.ts` lists every main-namespace article with its latest revision (`generator=allpages&prop=info`, 500 per request), downloads only the changed ones as wikitext (40 per request, `prop=revisions&rvslots=main`), and removes deleted ones; it refuses to shrink the index below half its size (a truncated listing must not wipe it). It sends a request every 2.5 s (about 20 a minute, inside the background half of the shared API budget) and waits 20 s when the budget is full anyway (`ApiBusyError`, at most 60 waits), instead of abandoning the refresh: at the old 1.5 s pace the budget refused it after 30 requests, the listing alone is about 60, so no refresh finished from 2026-10-27 until this was fixed. `clean.ts` turns wikitext into plain text (nested template removal with a small allowlist of display-only templates, tables flattened to rows, links and markup stripped) and splits it into ≤1000-character chunks by heading. `index.ts` stores them in an FTS5 table (porter stemming) in its own `wiki.db`, deliberately outside `bot.db` so it never bloats backups or migrations. `search()` puts the page whose title appears in the question first (shell pages like `Kuva Lich` fall through to `Kuva Lich/Main`), then bm25 matches (all words, then most words); patch-note pages are excluded unless the question mentions updates. `searchWiki`/`readWikiPage` in `warframe/wiki.ts` consult the index first once it has 500+ pages, and fall back to the live API, so every consumer (chat, `/wiki`, MCP) benefits. The `wiki-index` job checks every hour and refreshes once the last refresh is 6 h old (only changed pages are downloaded), so chat answers follow the wiki within a few hours. The wiki's full-page extracts are limited to one page per request, which is why this reads wikitext instead.
